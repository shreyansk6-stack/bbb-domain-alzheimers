To the Editorial Office,

I am requesting a waiver of the article processing charge for the above
submission, on grounds of financial need, under Springer Nature's standard
case-by-case waiver policy.

**The work is unfunded.** It received no grant from any funding agency in the
public, commercial or not-for-profit sectors. The study is entirely
computational: no compound reported in it was synthesised or assayed, and it drew
only on public data (ChEMBL, B3DB, PubChem) and open-source software. There was no
project budget from which a publication charge could be met.

**The authors are undergraduates.** This is student-led research carried out
without research funding or a departmental publication allowance. Absent a waiver,
the charge would fall on the authors personally.

**No institutional route covers this journal.** Davidson College participates in a
Springer Nature Read & Publish agreement through the Carolina Consortium, but that
agreement covers **hybrid** journals only. *Journal of Cheminformatics* is a fully
open-access BMC title and so falls outside it. The college does not operate a
separate open-access fund covering fully open-access charges, and the consortium
agreement is in any case scheduled to end on 31 December 2026, before this
manuscript could reasonably reach acceptance.

I would add that the paper's contribution is tied to its openness. Its argument
rests on a public pre-registration ([10.17605/OSF.IO/UQR62](https://doi.org/10.17605/OSF.IO/UQR62)),
a public analysis repository with a SHA-256-gated pipeline, a dated decision log
and a complete record of the literature search. The work is an audit of methods
other groups use, and it is most useful to those groups if they can read it
without a subscription.

I am glad to supply any confirmation of funding status the office requires, and I
understand that waiver decisions are made independently of editorial assessment
and that the manuscript will be judged on its merits either way.

Thank you for considering this request.
